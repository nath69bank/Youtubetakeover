# The 10 prompts, rewritten for Wirral AI

Paste any of these into Claude to regenerate or extend that part of the plan.
The brackets are already filled in for Wirral AI. Change them if the focus shifts.

**Context block:** paste this first, before any prompt, so every answer stays on-brand:

```
CONTEXT: I run wirral.ai, an AI consultancy based in Widnes, serving Wirral,
Liverpool, Manchester, Chester, Warrington, North Wales and the wider UK. We help
SMEs (5–300 staff: retail, clinics, hospitality, trades, membership bodies,
professional services) decide where AI genuinely fits, implement it, automate
repetitive work, train staff, and build AI-enabled websites and SEO. We've supported
40+ organisations. Our tone: practical, honest, no hype; we will say when AI is not
the answer. Pricing: free first conversation, then a fixed-price first piece of work.
The YouTube channel exists to generate enquiries (WhatsApp, website, free audit),
not AdSense income. Write in UK English.
```

## 1. Build your 90-day plan

```
Act as a world-class YouTube strategist and B2B lead-generation consultant. Build a
complete 90-day operating system to take my channel from zero to a reliable source of
business enquiries, and on track for YouTube Partner Program eligibility.
My niche is: AI, websites and SEO for UK small and medium business owners.
My audience is: owners and managers of UK SMEs (especially North West England and
North Wales) who know they "should be doing something with AI" or whose website
doesn't bring in work.
My resources are: [4–6 hours per week], [£0–£50/month budget], [phone or webcam,
USB mic, screen recorder].
My goal is: 10 qualified enquiries per month from YouTube by day 90.
Analyse my competitors and create my channel positioning, content pillars, posting
schedule, video formats, growth systems, retention framework, lead-generation plan
and a week-by-week execution calendar. Show me the milestones I should hit at day 30,
day 60 and day 90, for both channel metrics and leads.
```

## 2. Pick a niche that can win

```
Act as an elite market researcher and YouTube strategist. Help me choose the best
niche and positioning for a new channel. My interests are: practical AI for business,
web design, local SEO, automation. My skills are: AI consulting and implementation,
building websites and web apps, SEO, training staff. Compare 5 possible niches by
audience demand, competition level, search potential, ad friendliness, content
longevity, ways to stand out, AND likelihood each viewer becomes a paying client of a
UK AI/web consultancy. Pick the best one and explain why. Then create my channel
identity, my ideal viewer profile, 5 content categories, and 100 video ideas ranked by
growth potential and lead potential.
```

## 3. Spy on your competitors

```
Act as a YouTube competitor analyst. My niche is: AI, websites and SEO for UK small
business owners. List the 10 types of channel I should study (small, medium and large).
For each, tell me what to look for: top videos, title patterns, thumbnail style, video
length, upload rhythm and comment complaints. Then find 10 content gaps: topics UK
business owners want but nobody covers well (especially UK-specific angles: GDPR, UK
tools, local search, UK prices). Turn each gap into a video idea with a title and a
reason it can beat existing videos.
```

## 4. Write hooks and titles that get clicks

```
Act as a top 1% YouTube creator who studies viral videos. For my niche (AI, websites and
SEO for UK business owners), write 20 video ideas. For each one give me: 3 title
options, 1 thumbnail concept (max 4 words on screen), a hook for the first 15 seconds,
and a one-line reason someone would click. Use curiosity, a clear promise or a
surprise. Do not use fake clickbait. The video must deliver what the title promises.
Every idea must naturally lead to one of: a free download, a free website/AI audit, or
a WhatsApp chat with wirral.ai.
```

## 5. Script videos people finish

```
Act as a YouTube scriptwriter focused on audience retention. Write a full script for
this video idea: [video idea]. Length: [8–12] minutes. Use this structure: hook, quick
proof, story or setup, main points, a pattern interrupt every 60–90 seconds, a
mid-video teaser, and a strong ending that leads to my next video and one call to
action (wirral.ai free resource or WhatsApp). Write in simple spoken UK English.
Mark where to add B-roll, text on screen and sound effects. After the script, list
the moments viewers might leave and how you fixed each one.
```

## 6. Learn how the algorithm thinks

```
Act as a YouTube algorithm expert. Explain in plain English how impressions,
click-through rate, average view duration, audience retention, returning viewers,
search traffic, browse features and suggested videos work. Then build me a growth
strategy for a B2B channel with under 100 subscribers: how often to post, what to test
first, how to read the results, how to turn viewers into subscribers, AND how to turn
viewers into enquiries. End with a list of 5 common mistakes new business channels make.
```

## 7. Use Shorts to feed your channel

```
Act as a YouTube Shorts strategist. My niche is AI, websites and SEO for UK business
owners, and my long videos cover: practical AI tools, AI policy and risk, websites that
convert, local SEO and Google Business Profile, and automation. Create a 90-day Shorts
plan that brings new viewers to my long videos and to wirral.ai. Give me 30 Shorts
ideas, a hook for each, the best length, and a loop or ending that makes people watch
again. Show me how to turn each long video into 3 Shorts. Explain how Shorts views and
long-video watch hours are counted separately for the Partner Program, and which path
fits my channel better.
```

## 8. Set up an AI production workflow

```
Act as a professional YouTube production manager. Design an AI-powered workflow so I
can publish 1 long video and 3 Shorts a week in under 5 hours. Cover topic research,
scripting, recording (I will appear on camera and screen-record), editing, thumbnails,
publishing, data review, and repurposing into Shorts, TikTok, Reels and LinkedIn.
Recommend free or low-cost tools for each step. Give me templates and a quality
checklist. Include a rule to keep my content original and human, so it does not look
mass-produced or repetitive.
```

## 9. Plan how you will get paid

```
Act as a business strategist for creators who sell services. Design a monetisation
plan for my channel where the main income is client work for wirral.ai (AI consulting,
implementation, automation, websites, SEO, training). Include lead magnets, free audits,
WhatsApp/booking funnel, email newsletter, YouTube Partner Program and AdSense,
affiliate links (only for tools we genuinely use), sponsorships, digital products, paid
workshops, and a community. Tell me exactly when to add each: 0–500 subscribers,
500–1,000, and 1,000+. Include a checklist for the YPP application: 2-step verification,
no strikes, policy compliance, AdSense, and UK GDPR for collecting leads.
```

## 10. Review your numbers every week

```
Act as a YouTube data analyst. Create a weekly and monthly review system for my
channel. I will paste my analytics: impressions, CTR, average view duration, retention
graph notes, subscribers gained, traffic sources, engagement, AND lead data (link
clicks, downloads, WhatsApp chats, calls booked, clients won). Tell me what each number
means, what is good or bad for my channel size, and what to fix first. Find patterns in
my best videos. Give me a plan to improve my 3 weakest videos with new titles,
thumbnails or intros.
```
