# 9. How the channel pays: leads first, YouTube income second

*Output of prompt #9.*

## The honest maths

A B2B channel with a few thousand views a month earns pocket money from AdSense.
**One** client project from wirral.ai (fixed-price consulting, a website build, an
automation, training) is worth more than a year of ad revenue at that size.

So income comes in this order of importance:

1. **Client work** for wirral.ai ← the point of the channel
2. Paid workshops / training
3. Digital products (templates, prompt packs)
4. Affiliate links (only for tools we genuinely use)
5. YouTube ad revenue + memberships
6. Sponsorships (only from aligned UK B2B brands)

## When to add each income stream

### Stage 1: 0 to 500 subscribers (months 0–4)
Focus: **leads, not money from YouTube.**

| Add now | Why |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Free lead magnets** (3 by day 90: AI Readiness Checklist, AI Policy Template, Website & Google Check) | Converts viewers into contacts from video #1 |
| ✅ **WhatsApp CTA** (`wa.me/447368349702`) | Lowest-friction way for a business owner to say "can you look at mine?" |
| ✅ **Free website / Google Business Profile check** | Turns "Fix It Live" viewers into conversations |
| ✅ **Newsletter** (monthly, from week 5) | Owns the audience; YouTube can change, email doesn't |
| ✅ **UTM-tagged links + "how did you hear about us?" question** | Proves which videos bring clients |
| ⏸ Affiliate links | Only once you have 3–4 tools you'd genuinely recommend; add to descriptions with clear disclosure |
| ❌ AdSense, sponsorships, memberships | Not eligible / not worth the distraction yet |

### Stage 2: 500 to 1,000 subscribers (months 4–8)

| Add now | Why |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Affiliate links** for tools used in videos (hosting, Canva, automation tools, mics) | Small passive income; must be disclosed |
| ✅ **Paid workshop** (e.g. "AI for Your Business: Half-Day Workshop", online or in Liverpool/Chester) | Viewers who aren't ready for a project still pay to learn |
| ✅ **Digital product** (e.g. "AI Policy & Prompt Pack for UK SMEs", £19–£49) | Turns popular free resources into a paid upgrade |
| ✅ **Expanded YPP** (if 500 subs + 3,000 watch hours reached) | Unlocks memberships / Super Thanks. Low value for B2B but costs nothing |
| ✅ **Case-study videos** from clients won via YouTube | Social proof that compounds leads |

### Stage 3: 1,000+ subscribers (months 8+)

| Add now | Why |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Full YPP + AdSense** (once 4,000 watch hours reached) | Ad revenue on a B2B audience pays relatively well per view |
| ✅ **Sponsorships** from aligned UK B2B brands (accounting software, business banks, hosting) | Only if we'd use the product; never hurts trust |
| ✅ **Online course** (e.g. "Practical AI for UK SMEs") | Only once workshops prove demand and content |
| ✅ **Community** (e.g. a free or paid group for North West business owners using AI) | Retention + referrals; start free, add paid tier later |
| ✅ **Memberships** (channel memberships or a "monthly AI clinic") | Recurring income from the most engaged viewers |

## YouTube Partner Program application checklist

**Eligibility** *(check the current YPP page before applying; thresholds change)*
- [ ] Channel is in an eligible country (UK ✔)
- [ ] **500 subs + 3 uploads in 90 days + 3,000 watch hours/12 months** (expanded), or
- [ ] **1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch hours/12 months** (full, ad revenue)
- [ ] (Shorts paths: 3M or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. Not our route, see [07](../content/07-shorts-plan.md))

**Account security & standing**
- [ ] **2-Step Verification** turned on for the Google account
- [ ] Channel verified (phone) and advanced features enabled
- [ ] **No active Community Guidelines strikes**
- [ ] Only one AdSense account per person/business; ready to link

**Policy compliance**
- [ ] All content original and human-led (see [originality rule](../operations/08-production-workflow.md#the-originality-rule-human-first-ai-second))
- [ ] No music, footage or images we don't have rights to
- [ ] Advertiser-friendly: no misleading claims, no shocking thumbnails
- [ ] Paid promotion / sponsorship boxes ticked where relevant
- [ ] Synthetic/altered content disclosed where realistic
- [ ] Videos meet the YouTube monetisation policies (reviewers watch your main theme, most-viewed and newest videos, so make sure those are strong)

**UK GDPR & marketing rules for collecting leads**
- [ ] Privacy notice on every lead-capture page (what you collect, why, how long, who to contact)
- [ ] Newsletter sign-up uses **opt-in consent** (unticked box) and every email has an unsubscribe link (PECR)
- [ ] Only collect what you need (name, email, business, website is plenty)
- [ ] Lead data stored in a business system with access controls, not a personal spreadsheet
- [ ] WhatsApp conversations: don't add people to broadcast lists without consent
- [ ] Affiliate links and sponsorships **clearly labelled** in the video and description (ASA/CMA guidance)
- [ ] Never show client data or name a client without written permission
- [ ] Claims about results are true and evidenced (CAP Code)

## Lead targets & tracking

| Metric | Where it's tracked | Day 90 target |
|---|---|---|
| Description link clicks (UTM) | Website analytics → source = youtube | 150+/month |
| Lead magnet downloads | Email tool / form | 30–80/month |

| Calls booked | Calendar / CRM | 4–8/month |
| Clients won (attributed) | CRM field "Source: YouTube + video #" | 2–4 in first 90 days |
| Revenue attributed | CRM | Track from day 1 |

Ask every new enquiry: **"How did you find us?"** and if it's YouTube, **"which video?"**
