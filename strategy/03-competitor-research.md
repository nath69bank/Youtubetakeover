**Where to find complaints fast:** sort comments by *Top*, search the page for
"UK", "what about", "doesn't work", "too complicated", "how much", "GDPR".

## Patterns already visible in the niche

- **Almost everything is American.** US prices, US tools, US law. UK viewers ask
  "does this work in the UK?" constantly.
- **Too technical for owners.** Automation and SEO channels speak to marketers and
  developers, not to the plumber or practice manager.
- **Hype-heavy AI content.** "Replace your staff with AI" thumbnails. Comments
  show distrust and fatigue.
- **No real client work shown.** Agencies talk *about* results but rarely show the
  build on screen.
- **No one talks about risk sensibly.** It's either ignored or scary. Practical,
  calm GDPR/policy guidance is rare.
