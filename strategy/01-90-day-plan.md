# 1. The 90-day operating system

*Output of prompt #1.*

**Goal by day 90:** a channel that produces **~10 qualified enquiries a month**, with
13 long videos and ~39 Shorts published, on a realistic path to Partner Program
eligibility in months 6–12.

**Resources assumed:** 4–6 hours/week · £0–£50/month · phone or webcam, USB mic, screen recorder.

## Video formats

| Format | Length | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **How-to / walkthrough** | 8–12 min | Search traffic, evergreen | "Google Business Profile: complete setup" |
| **Fix It Live** (signature) | 12–18 min | Trust + proof + leads | Live local SEO makeover |
| **Teardown / audit** | 8–12 min | Shows expertise, triggers "do mine" | "3 local websites, what I'd change" |
| **Honest test** | 8–12 min | Browse/suggested traffic | "I gave ChatGPT my inbox for a week" |
| **Explainer / opinion** | 6–10 min | Positioning, trust | "When AI is NOT the answer" |
| **Shorts** | 20–45 s | Discovery | One tip, one mistake, one result |

## Growth systems

1. **Search-first titles.** Every long video targets a phrase people type
   (check YouTube autocomplete + Google "People also ask").
2. **Series & playlists.** "AI for [Sector]", "Fix It Live", "Get Found on Google".
   Playlists raise session time.
3. **Shorts → long video bridge.** Each Short ends pointing to the full video
   (use the *related video* link on Shorts).
4. **Website ↔ video loop.** Embed each video on the matching wirral.ai page or
   insight article; link that page in the description. Helps both rankings.
5. **Local distribution.** Share each video in 3 places: LinkedIn, local business
   Facebook groups (Wirral/Liverpool/Chester business networks), and the newsletter.
6. **Collab with locals.** Feature real local businesses (with permission) in Fix It
   Live. They share it, so their audience = your audience.

## Retention framework (every long video)

- **0–15 s:** Hook. State the problem + the payoff. No logo intro, no "hi guys".
- **15–45 s:** Proof. Show the result first (before/after, number, screen).
- **Every 60–90 s:** Pattern interrupt: cut to screen, zoom, text pop, B-roll, question.
- **Midpoint:** Teaser: "The last tip is the one most businesses miss."
- **Ending:** No "thanks for watching" fade. Go straight to: one CTA + end screen to
  the next video.

## Milestones

These are targets for a new B2B channel posting weekly. Treat them as checkpoints
to diagnose, not promises.

| | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long videos published | 4 | 8 | 13 |
| Shorts published | 12 | 24 | 39 |
| Subscribers | 50–150 | 150–350 | 300–700 |
| Public watch hours (cumulative) | 50–150 | 200–500 | 500–1,200 |
| Avg CTR (long videos) | 3–5% | 4–6% | 5–7% |
| Avg % viewed (long) | 30%+ | 35%+ | 40%+ |
| Lead magnet downloads / month | 5–15 | 15–40 | 30–80 |
| **Enquiries (WhatsApp/form/calls) / month** | 1–2 | 3–6 | **8–12** |
| Paying clients attributed to YouTube | 0–1 | 1–2 | 2–4 |

**If you're behind at day 30:** fix CTR first (titles/thumbnails).
**At day 60:** fix retention (first 30 seconds).
**At day 90:** fix conversion (CTA, lead magnet, landing page).
