### Winner: E. AI, websites & SEO for UK business owners

- **Lead fit is the whole game.** Niches A–C attract creators, students and other
  agencies, who will never hire us. E attracts the person who signs the cheque.
- **The UK angle is wide open.** Almost all AI/SEO content is American: US tools, US
  prices, no GDPR, no ICO, "zip codes". A UK voice talking about Companies House, HMRC,
  UK GDPR, the ICO, "Google Business Profile in Birkenhead" stands out instantly.
- **We can prove it.** 40+ organisations supported, live builds (e.g. *The Order Block*),
  and real screen recordings. Most AI channels can't show client work.
- **Honesty is the differentiator.** The market is drowning in "AI will 10x your
  business". "Here's where AI won't help you" is rare, and it builds trust fast.
- **Evergreen with a news layer.** Local SEO, websites and AI policy last for years;
  occasional "what this AI update means for your business" videos ride news spikes.

## Ideal viewer profile

**Name:** "Busy Bev" / "Practical Pete" (use as a writing target, not in videos)

- **Who:** Owner, director or operations manager of a UK business with 5–150 staff.
  Clinic, salon, trades firm, estate agent, accountant, solicitor, gym, hotel, shop,
  membership body, charity.
- **Where:** North West England & North Wales first; UK-wide second.
- **Age:** 35–60. Watches YouTube on a laptop at lunch or on the sofa at night.
- **Tech confidence:** Uses email, WhatsApp, maybe Xero and Canva. Has tried ChatGPT.
  Not a developer.
- **Pains:**
  - "Everyone says AI, but what do I actually *do* with it on Monday?"
  - "Our website looks dated and doesn't bring in enquiries."
  - "We don't show up on Google when people search for what we do round here."
  - "My team are drowning in admin."
  - "Staff are pasting stuff into ChatGPT and I don't know if that's a problem."
- **Fears:** Wasting money on hype; being ripped off by an agency; data breaches; being
  left behind by competitors.
- **Wants:** Clear steps, UK-relevant examples, honest cost estimates, someone local
  they can trust.
- **Trigger to buy:** They've tried it themselves, got 60% of the way, and want someone
  to finish it properly.
