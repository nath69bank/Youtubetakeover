# Lead magnets and the offer ladder

The channel is the top of the funnel. These free resources turn viewers into
contacts, and contacts into conversations.

## The offer ladder

| Step | Offer | Cost to viewer | What we capture | Leads to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | YouTube video | Free | n/a | Lead magnet |
| 2 | **Downloadable resource** (checklist, template, prompts) | Free | Name + email (+ business, optional) | Newsletter, check |
| 3 | **Free check** (website, Google profile or AI readiness, 3 fixes sent back) | Free | Website URL, WhatsApp/email | Conversation |
| 4 | **Free first conversation** | Free | Business details | Proposal |
| 5 | **Fixed-price first piece of work** | Paid | Client | Ongoing work |
| (side) | Workshop / product pack | £ | Customer | Step 5 |

## The 5 lead magnets (build in this order)

### 1. AI Readiness Checklist ✅ (already exists on the site)
- **Source:** wirral.ai/insights/ai-readiness-checklist. Turn it into a 1-page PDF
  with a score box (0–8).
- **Used in videos:** #1, #11, #15, #21
- **Landing page headline:** *Is your business actually ready for AI? 8 questions, 5 minutes.*
- **After download:** email with result bands → "Scored under 5? Reply and we'll tell
  you what to fix first."

### 2. Website & Google Check (free, done-for-you)
- **Format:** viewer sends their website and/or Google Business Profile link (WhatsApp
  or form); we reply within 2 working days with **3 specific fixes** (screen recording
  or short written list).
- **Used in videos:** #2, #3, #6, #7, #9, #13, #19
- **CTA line:** "Send us your website on WhatsApp and we'll send back three fixes. Free, no sales call."
- **Capacity limit:** cap at ~10/week; say "first 10 each week" (true scarcity, not fake).
- **Why it works:** a free check is the fastest route from "watched a video" to "talking to us".

### 3. One-Page AI Policy Template
- **Format:** editable Google Doc / Word template, one page, with the 3 rules from
  script 001 expanded: never-share data list, human-check rule, approved tools, who to ask.
- **Source:** wirral.ai/insights/ai-policy-uk-smes
- **Used in videos:** #4, #14, #27
- **After download:** "Want us to tailor it and run a 1-hour staff session? Reply."

### 4. 10 AI Prompts for UK Business Owners
- **Format:** 1–2 page PDF. Full text below, ready to design in Canva.
- **Used in videos:** #5, #16–18 Shorts, #20
- **Meta-angle for a video:** "Someone posted 10 prompts to grow a YouTube channel.
  Here are 10 to grow a *business*."

### 5. Google Business Profile Checklist
- **Format:** 1-page checklist (categories, services, description, photos, reviews,
  posts, Q&A, hours, NAP consistency).
- **Used in videos:** #2, #9, #22, #23

## Lead magnet #4: full text

> **10 AI Prompts for UK Business Owners** · from wirral.ai
>
> Use a **business** AI account. Never paste customer personal data, card details
> or health information. A human checks everything before it goes to a customer.

**1. Write a quote**
> Act as an experienced [trade/service] business in [town], UK. Write a clear,
> friendly quote for [customer need]. Include: scope of work, what's not included,
> price of £[amount] + VAT, timescale, payment terms, and next step. Keep it under
> 250 words. UK English.

**2. Reply to a complaint**
> Here's a customer complaint (names removed): [paste]. Write a calm, professional
> reply that acknowledges the problem, doesn't admit legal liability, explains what
> we'll do next, and offers a phone call. Warm, not grovelling. Under 150 words.

**3. Reply to a Google review**
> Write a reply to this [positive/negative] Google review: [paste]. Thank them by
> first name, mention one specific detail, include "[service] in [town]" naturally
> once, keep it under 60 words. No generic phrases like "valued customer".

**4. Write a job advert**
> Write a job advert for a [role] at a [size/type] business in [town]. Salary £[x].
> Include what the day looks like, who'd love it, who wouldn't, and how to apply.
> Plain English, inclusive language, no clichés like "rockstar" or "fast-paced".

**5. Turn notes into an email**
> Turn these rough meeting notes into a clear follow-up email with actions, owners
> and dates: [paste notes, personal data removed].

**6. Improve your website homepage**
> Here is my homepage text: [paste]. I'm a [business] in [town] serving [customers].
> Rewrite the top section so a stranger understands in 5 seconds what we do, who
> for, where, and what to do next. Give me 3 headline options and 1 call to action.

**7. Write your Google Business Profile description**
> Write a 700-character Google Business Profile description for [business] in
> [town], offering [services] to [customers]. Mention areas served ([list]). Natural,
> not keyword-stuffed. No links or phone numbers (Google doesn't allow them).

**8. Find what to automate**
> Here's a list of the tasks my team does each week, with rough hours: [paste]. Rank
> them by how suitable they are for automation or AI, explain why, and flag any
> that involve personal data and need extra care.

**9. Draft a simple policy**
> Draft a one-page [AI use / social media / remote working] policy for a UK business
> with [x] staff. Plain English, practical, headings, under 400 words. Note anything
> I should check with a professional (e.g. HR or legal).

**10. Plan a month of content**
> I run a [business] in [town]. My customers are [who] and they often ask [common
> questions]. Create a 4-week plan with 2 social posts and 1 short video idea per
> week, each answering a real customer question.
