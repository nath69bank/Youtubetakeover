# YouTube Takeover: Wirral AI lead-magnet channel

The full operating system for a YouTube channel that turns UK business owners into
enquiries for **wirral.ai**: AI consulting and implementation, automation,
websites and SEO.

It's built from the 10-prompt "YouTube growth" framework. Each prompt has been
rewritten for Wirral AI, then **run and filled in**, so you have both:

1. **The prompts** (`prompts/`), ready to paste into Claude whenever you want to
   regenerate or extend any part.
2. **The finished output** (`strategy/`, `content/`, `operations/`, `funnel/`):
   a plan you can start filming from this week.

> **Channel in one line:** Practical, no-hype videos showing UK business owners
> how to use AI, websites and SEO to win more customers. Made by people who
> actually build it.

---

## Why a lead-magnet channel works differently

Most YouTube advice optimises for views and AdSense. This channel doesn't. One
local business owner who books a call is worth more than 100,000 views from
people who will never buy. So:

- **Success metric = enquiries**, not subscribers. Views and subscribers still
  matter, but only as leading indicators.
- **Small and specific beats big and broad.** "How a Wirral plumber gets found on
  Google" beats "SEO explained".
- **Every video has one job:** solve one real problem completely, then offer
  the next step (a free download, a free audit, or a chat on WhatsApp).

## Brand rules (non-negotiable)

- **No hype.** Wirral AI is positioned against "AI will 10x your business" content.
  Honest beats exciting. Say when AI is *not* the answer.
- **No fake results.** Only show real numbers, real clients (with permission) and
  real builds. UK advertising rules (CAP Code) apply to claims made in videos.
- **Show, don't tell.** Screen recordings of real tools beat talking heads.
- **Local is a feature.** Mention Wirral, Liverpool, Chester, Manchester, North Wales
  by name. It helps search and it builds trust.
- **Every video ends with one CTA**, not three.
