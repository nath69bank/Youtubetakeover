# 7. 90-day Shorts plan

*Output of prompt #7.* Shorts are for **discovery**: they put Wirral AI in front of
business owners who'd never search for us. Every Short points to a long video or a
free resource.

## Rules for every Short

- **Length:** 20–45 s (sweet spot ~30 s). One idea only.
- **First 1 second:** text on screen + spoken hook at the same time. No intro.
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, face + screen split, burned-in captions (most watch muted).
- **Ending:** either a **loop** (last line flows into the first) or a **bridge** ("full
  walkthrough in the linked video").
- **Link it:** use the *Related video* field on every Short to link the long video.
- **Repost to:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn (native upload, not a link),
  Facebook Reels. Remove other platforms' watermarks before reposting.

## How to turn one long video into 3 Shorts

| Short | What to cut | Hook formula |
|---|---|---|
| **A: The tip** | The single most useful 30 s | "Do this before you [action]…" |
| **B: The mistake** | The "don't do this" moment | "Stop [common mistake]. Here's why." |
| **C: The result** | The before/after, number, or surprising answer | "We [did X]. This happened." |

### 90-day schedule

- **Weeks 1–13:** 3 Shorts/week (Wed, Fri, Sun). Mostly cut from that week's long video
  (type A/B/C above), topped up from this list.
- **Day 30:** check which hooks got >70% "viewed vs swiped away". Do more of that style.
- **Day 60:** turn the top 3 Shorts into full long videos if they don't have one yet.
- **Day 90:** keep the 2 best Short formats as recurring series (e.g. "Prompt of the
  week", "5-second website test").

## Shorts views vs watch hours for the Partner Program

YouTube counts these **separately**. You qualify via one path *or* the other. They
don't add together.

| Tier (UK) | Subscribers | Path A: long-form | Path B: Shorts | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Expanded YPP** | 500 | 3,000 public watch hours in last 12 months | 3M public Shorts views in last 90 days | Memberships, Super Thanks/Chat, shopping |
| **Full YPP** | 1,000 | 4,000 public watch hours in last 12 months | 10M public Shorts views in last 90 days | Ad revenue (long + Shorts) |

Both tiers also require 3 public uploads in the last 90 days. *(Thresholds as
published by YouTube; check the current YPP page before applying, since they change.)*

**Key detail:** views of Shorts in the Shorts feed **do not** count toward the
4,000 watch hours. Only long-form public watch time does.

### Which path fits Wirral AI?

**Path A (long-form watch hours). Clearly.**

- Our leads come from long videos: search traffic, trust built over 10 minutes.
- 4,000 hours ≈ 24,000 views × 10 min average. Very achievable over 12 months
  with an evergreen search library.
- 10M Shorts views in 90 days is a viral-entertainment target, the wrong game for a
  B2B consultancy.

So Shorts' job is **not** to qualify for YPP. It's to **send viewers into long videos**
(which builds watch hours *and* leads).
