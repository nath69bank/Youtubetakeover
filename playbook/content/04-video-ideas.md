# 4. 20 video ideas: titles, thumbnails, hooks

*Output of prompt #4.* Every idea delivers exactly what the title promises, with
no fake clickbait. Each ends with one CTA pointing to a free resource or a chat.

**Thumbnail rules:** max 4 words · face with a real expression + one clear visual
(screen, before/after, map pin) · high contrast · readable at phone size · no
red arrows pointing at nothing.
