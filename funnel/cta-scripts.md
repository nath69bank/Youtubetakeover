# CTA lines (say one per video)

Rules: **one** CTA per video · say it out loud **and** put it in description line 1 **and**
the pinned comment · match it to the topic · make the next step tiny.

## Mid-video soft mention (around 40–60%)
- "I've put all ten of these prompts in a free one-pager. It's in the description,
  so don't pause now, grab it at the end."
- "There's a free version of this checklist linked below. Keep watching, though,
  because the next bit is the one people get wrong."

## End-of-video CTAs by lead magnet

**AI Readiness Checklist**
> "If you want to check whether your business is actually ready for this, we've got
> a free AI Readiness Checklist. Eight questions, five minutes. It's the first link
> in the description."

**AI Policy Template**
> "You can download our one-page AI policy template free. It's the same one we start
> with for clients. Link's in the description and the pinned comment."

**10 AI Prompts**
> "All ten prompts are in a free one-page download. Copy them, change the brackets,
> use them today. First link in the description."

**Website & Google Check**
> "If you want us to look at yours, send your website or Google profile to us on
> WhatsApp. The number's in the description. We'll send back three specific fixes.
> Free, no sales call. We do the first ten each week."

**Free conversation (bottom-of-funnel videos: costs, choosing a consultant, build vs buy)**
> "If you're at the point of actually deciding, the first conversation with us is
> free. No hourly rates, no retainers. If we work together, it's a fixed price agreed
> up front. WhatsApp's in the description."

## Subscribe lines (after value is delivered, ~60–70%)
- "Every Tuesday I fix one real business's website, Google profile or workflow. If
  you want to see whose is next, subscribe."
- "This is part of a series on getting found on Google. Subscribe and you'll get the
  next one."

## Shorts endings
- Bridge: "Full walkthrough's in the linked video ⬆️"
- Loop: end mid-sentence that the first line completes.
- Resource: "Free template, link on the channel."

## Lines to never use
- ❌ "Smash that like button"
- ❌ "Link in bio" (YouTube has no bio)
- ❌ "Limited spots!" (unless genuinely true, as with the 10 free checks/week)
- ❌ Any result we can't prove ("triple your leads")
